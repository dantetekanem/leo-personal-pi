## Dates and names
- Studio founded November 1999 by Eric Jordan, Tony Novak and John Carroll [src: Web Design Museum history page].
- V3 Expansions: 2001. V4 Prophecy: copyright 2003 in the movie, news dated early 2004, archive says 2004.
  V5 Attractor: 2006 ("Release Five - 2006"), content through 2009. V2 was "Rebirth" (named in the V4 and V5
  archive menus [asset]). A V6 was "in the works" in 2009 [src: creativebloq] but never shipped; no reliable source
  names a V6 or V7 ("Ascension" and "Nexus" appear only in low-reliability summaries).

## Eric Jordan, in his words [src: creativebloq 2009 unless noted]
- "2Advanced's different versions are never just websites to me, they're instalments in a kind of story or saga
  that I'm trying to bring to the world."
- "It's an idea that everything is always moving forward, always changing, always dynamic."
- "I was able to bring all these things to bear with sound and motion."
- "Music and design are very much tied together, I think."
- "To be able to give a sense of 3D on the web is something that I've always dreamed about."
- "I try to offer them a beacon of light to show them that there's hope." [inf: the optimism is part of the
  taste; the taglines promise a hopeful future, not a dystopia.]
- "Everyone gets their inspiration from the outside world. The only difference is what the person chooses to
  illuminate."
- The studio describes itself today as "a world-building brand and experimental design platform" [src: About].
